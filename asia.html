Discover Asia

Tropical islands, bustling megacities, and the lowest cost of living.

Bali, Indonesia

The spiritual hub of digital nomads.

Bali offers a unique blend of lush rice terraces, ancient temples, and a massive community of remote workers. Areas like Canggu and Ubud are packed with co-working spaces, yoga studios, and healthy cafes.

  • Average Cost: $1,200/month
  • Visa: 30-day Visa on Arrival
  • Vibe: Relaxed & Spiritual

Bangkok, Thailand

The ultimate megacity experience.

Bangkok is a sensory overload in the best way possible. It boasts world-class street food, incredible rooftop bars, and some of the fastest internet speeds in Southeast Asia. It's perfect for nomads who love city energy.

  • Average Cost: $1,000/month
  • Visa: 30-day Visa Exemption
  • Vibe: Energetic & Urban

Chiang Mai, Thailand

The original digital nomad capital.

Located in the mountains of Northern Thailand, Chiang Mai is famous for its low cost of living, incredible food, and digital nomad infrastructure. It's cooler, quieter, and more cultural than Bangkok.

  • Average Cost: $800/month
  • Visa: 30-day Visa Exemption
  • Vibe: Cultural & Affordable
