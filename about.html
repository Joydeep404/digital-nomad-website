Our Journey

How NomadGuide started and where we are going.

The Idea (2022)

A group of remote workers realized there was no single, reliable place to find safe, affordable, and fast-WiFi destinations.

First Launch (2023)

We launched our beta version with just 10 cities. The community grew rapidly, reaching 5,000 active nomads in the first month.

Going Global (2024)

Expanded to over 50 countries. Added interactive budget calculators and visa guides to help nomads plan better.

Today & Beyond (2026)

We are now a thriving community of 50,000+ digital nomads. Our mission is to make location independence accessible to everyone.
