City Deep Dives

Mexico City, Mexico

The ultimate megacity experience in the Americas.

CDMX is a massive metropolis with world-class food, incredible art scenes, and a huge expat community. Neighborhoods like Roma and Condesa are packed with cafes perfect for remote work.

  • Average Cost: $1,500/month
  • Visa: 180-day Tourist Card (FMM)
  • Vibe: Urban, Artistic, & Foodie

Medellin, Colombia

The "City of Eternal Spring".

Medellin boasts perfect weather year-round, incredibly affordable living, and a massive digital nomad hub in the El Poblado neighborhood. It's safe, modern, and full of energy.

  • Average Cost: $1,100/month
  • Visa: 90-day Tourist Stamp
  • Vibe: Relaxed, Social, & Affordable

Buenos Aires, Argentina

European architecture with a Latin soul.

Buenos Aires offers passionate tango culture, incredible steak, and a booming tech scene. Thanks to the current exchange rate, it is one of the most affordable major cities in the world right now.

  • Average Cost: $900/month
  • Visa: 90-day Tourist Visa
  • Vibe: Cultural, Nightlife, & Historic
